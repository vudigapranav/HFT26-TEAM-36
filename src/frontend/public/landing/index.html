TorqueCode is Team PAARK’s portfolio-driven adaptive coding and technical assessment project for CBIT Hacktoberfest ’26.

The TypeScript backend brings together portfolio intake, onboarding, timed assessments, sandbox execution, guarded assistance, and event-based reporting. Correctness comes from tests; the AI assistant is there to guide, not grade.
